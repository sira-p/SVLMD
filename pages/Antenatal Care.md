- # First ANC Visit
	- ## History
		- Personal history: อาชีพ, การศึกษา, ศาสนา, ภูมิลำเนา
		- Family history: โรคในครอบครัว, โรคพันธุกรรม
		- Surgical history
		- Smoking, drinking, illicit drug use
		- Allergy
		- Contraception
		- [[LMP]] (date, duration), PMP (previous menstrual period), regularity, interval
		- Obstetrics history
			- Gravida/para
			- Year, sex, birth weight, antepartum complications, delivery method, indication for operative delivery, postpartum complications
			- Curettage
		- Present illness
	- ## Physical Examination
		- Height, pre-pregnant weight, pre-pregnant [[BMI]]
		- CVS, RS, thyroid, breasts, pelvic examination
	- ## Investigation
		- ### ANC Lab 1
			- Blood group: [[ABO]], [[Rh]]
			- [[Hb]], [[Hct]], ±[[Plt]]
			- Serology: [[Treponemal Test]] , [[HBsAg]], [[anti-HIV]]
			- [[Anti-HIV]] สามี
			- [[Rubella]] immune status
			- [[HbA1c]]
			- [[UA]]
			- [[Hemoglobin Typing]] for [[Beta-thalassemia]] (ผู้ป่วย, สามี)
				- If negative but low MCV: [[PCR]] for [[Alpha-thal-1]]
				- Couple at risk?
			- [[MCV]], [[MCHC]]
			- Assess [[Preeclampsia]], [[GDM]], [[Preterm]] risk
		- ### Ultrasound
			- [[Crown-rump Length]]
			- [[EDC]] by LMP/US at GA
